Fig. 2 · Differentiable Dynamics Rollout & Backprop

Analytical Policy Gradients (APG) Through Physics

Unlike sample-inefficient model-free RL (e.g., PPO) or expensive expert imitation, the system dynamics sk+1 = f(sk, uk) are differentiable, allowing direct backpropagation of step losses Lk through time.

Key Advantage: Even a single trajectory rollout provides an exact analytical gradient ∂L/∂θ via Backpropagation Through Time (BPTT) with temporal gradient decay (batch size 16, 10K iterations).

Compensating for 15% Real-World Thrust Mismatch

Due to battery voltage drop and airflow, the real drone needs ~1.15g commanded thrust just to hover. Without gravity randomization (Fig. 9a), the policy outputs 1.0g and drops to the ground. With g ∼ N(9.81, 1.52) (Fig. 9b), it adapts thrust up to 1.3g via velocity feedback and holds the red setpoint!
