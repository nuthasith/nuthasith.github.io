Humanoids

  • SuperMimic, world models, and training humanoids

    Start here. Four parts covering the RL loop, rewards, PD control and world models, with a worked number for every equation.

  • SuperMimic reproductionNOTES

    A Unitree G1 motion tracker trained through a learned world model, rebuilt from the paper alone.

  • Puppeteer: a world model that pulls the strings

    A second world-model paper explained the same way. Builds on the SuperMimic primer.

  • HALO: humanoid home skills learned entirely in simulation

    A G1 follows requests like "crouch under the table, then sit on the chair" in unseen homes, trained only on simulated data.

  • Whole-body humanoid locomotion via motion generation and tracking

    A diffusion model sketches the next 0.5 s of motion from the terrain ahead, and an RL tracker turns it into joint commands.

  • Humanoid mobility via attention-based contacts

    Predict only where 9 body parts should touch the terrain next, and let RL work out how to get there. One G1 policy climbs, crawls and vaults.

Drones

  • SkyDreamer: Dreamer world models and racing drones from pixels

    Four parts, read in order. Part 4 compares it with SuperMimic.

  • DiffDreamer

    Coupling world models with differentiable simulation for agile quadrotor control.

  • Simple but stable, fast and safe

    End-to-end obstacle avoidance trained through a high-fidelity differentiable simulator.

  • Learning agile quadrotor flight in the real world

    A drone starts with a slow, safe policy, learns what its physics model gets wrong, and speeds itself up while flying.

  • DiffCoord

    Tuning a multi-drone planner with exact gradients computed through the planner itself.

  • Safe multi-agent navigation via constrained HJB-informed learning

    HJB-GNN: one closed-form weight, λ, that balances goal-reaching and collision safety for many drones.

  • Quadrotor navigation using RL with privileged information

    Time-of-arrival maps, used only in training, teach a drone to turn and fly around large walls instead of getting stuck. Tested in 1,350 simulated trials and 20 outdoor flights.

Safety and control

  • CBF-RL and CLF-RL

    Two papers that use control barrier and Lyapunov functions only during RL training, so the robot runs a plain policy.

  • Learn how wrong the model is, then patch the safety rules

    Choi et al., RL for safety-critical control under model uncertainty using CLFs and CBFs.

  • TOA-CBF paper guide3-PART GUIDE

    The RA-L 2026 paper explained, with a matching reproduction manual and code guide.

  • A decade of learning-based controlSURVEY

    Schoellig et al. Three method families (learned dynamics, direct data-driven control, and RL with Bayesian optimisation) and the stability and safety ideas behind them.

  • PS2-RL: provably safe, yet scalable RL

    Yun, Li and Azizan. Learn a "get home safely" backup policy first, then train the task policy through a QP layer that only allows inputs the backup can recover from. 100% safe on a 10-state quadrotor powerloop.
